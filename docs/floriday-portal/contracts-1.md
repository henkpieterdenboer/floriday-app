# Contracts

Contracts are used for long term agreements between suppliers and customers. Contracts can be created by both suppliers and customers. These contracts generate customer specific supply lines, which both the supplier and the customer may call on by issuing blanket orders (AKA standing orders). When a blanket order is issued and approved by the other party, a sales order is created. All contract trade agreements are subject to a purchase obligation.

For the business rules concerning contracts, please read [Contracts](https://developer.floriday.io/docs/contracts-3).
