# Selected trade items

## Target audience

* Customer organizations

<br />

## Purpose

* Enable a customer organization to select/deselect and apply favorite trade items.

<br />

## Guidance

**Selected trade items:**

* Can only be selected or deselected by a customer organization and supplier organizations(app only) of the trade-items;
* Can not be deselected by supplier organizations if selected by customer organizations;
* Applies to trade items of selected supplier organizations;
* Can be seen by the supplier;
* Can be notified to the supplier.

<br />

**Packing configuration selections:**

* Enable a customer organization to set trade item packing configs as primary. If set to primary, other packing configs in the trade item are set to secundary;
* Can only be enabled and disabled by a customer organization;
* Applies to trade items of selected supplier organizations;
* Can not be seen by the supplier.

<br />

> 📘 Methods of selecting trade items
>
> There are three ways to select trade items as a customer:
>
> * Select trade items on the buyers platform Flordiday by marking the trade item as a favourite;
> * Enable suppliers to mark their trade items as your selected trade items by enabling this in the system settings on the Floriday platform;
> * Select trade items via the Floriday API.
